Why these studies? Neobe is preparing NEO22 for a first human trial. These seven selected reports on other products show how teams tested whether bacteria reached human tumours. Each result is specific to its route, assay and sampling time. Neobe’s development plans ↗

How to read the counts. Positive means at least one positive specimen per person for the stated test. A missing result contributes to the treated count only. DNA detection, signs on imaging and relative tissue enrichment answer different questions from a positive culture. The selection is illustrative and provides no estimate for NEO22.
